
## 2024-05-24 - Escape to Close & Aria Controls
**Learning:** Custom overlay menus must provide keyboard accessibility such as pressing the `Escape` key to close. Also, custom toggle buttons that control elements need the `aria-controls` attribute linking them to their target container to be fully accessible for screen readers.
**Action:** Always verify `Escape` handling and `aria-controls` for any custom toggles or overlay menus introduced or modified.
