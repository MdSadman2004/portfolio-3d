## 2026-10-26 - CollatzOracle Toggle Buttons\n**Learning:** The custom interactive modes (ORBIT/DISTRIBUTION) and range toggles in CollatzOracle were functioning as visually selected toggles but lacked programmatic state for screen readers.\n**Action:** Applied `aria-pressed` to buttons acting as toggles and grouped them using `role="group"` and `aria-label` to ensure the UI structure is semantically clear to assistive technologies.
